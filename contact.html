Contact Learnix

Have a question, suggestion or feedback? We'd love to hear from you.

1. Get in Touch

If you have questions about Learnix, our learning resources or any of the available study tools, you can contact us.

We also welcome suggestions that can help us improve the learning experience for students.

2. Questions and Feedback

You can contact Learnix if you notice an incorrect explanation, broken link, technical problem or other issue on the website.

When contacting us, please clearly explain the issue and mention the page or feature where you found it.

3. Suggestions

We are interested in suggestions for new study topics, practice questions, coding exercises, placement resources and useful student tools.

Your feedback can help us make Learnix more useful and easier to use.

4. Contact Information

For questions, feedback or suggestions, please contact the Learnix team by email.

Email: learnix.student@gmail.com

5. Response Time

We try to review messages and respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the number and nature of requests.

6. Before Contacting Us

Please do not send passwords, payment information, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information by email.

Learnix does not require such information to provide its general educational resources.
