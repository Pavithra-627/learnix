Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Learnix handles information when you use our website and learning resources.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Learnix. Learnix is a free student learning platform that provides educational resources, study tools, coding practice, quizzes, communication practice and career preparation resources.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you visit Learnix and explains how that information may be used.

By using Learnix, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Learnix is designed to provide learning resources without requiring users to create a paid account.

Some features may allow you to enter information such as your name or learning preferences. Information entered into such features is used only to provide or improve the requested functionality.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from students through the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided through Learnix features may be used to operate the website, provide learning tools, improve the user experience and maintain website functionality.

We may also use technical information, such as browser type, device information and general usage information, to understand how visitors use the website and to improve our services.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Learnix and third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies. Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, understand website usage and support advertising services.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

Learnix may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on eligible pages of the website.

Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize advertising, where applicable.

Advertising practices may change as Learnix develops. Users should review Google's privacy and advertising information for more details about how Google handles data.

6. Third-Party Services

Learnix may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, advertising or other technical functions.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Storage and Security

Learnix takes reasonable steps to protect information used by the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Some Learnix features may store information locally in your browser. Such information may remain on your device until you clear your browser storage or remove the relevant data.

8. Children's Privacy

Learnix provides general educational resources for students. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information from children.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through the website may contact us so that we can review the request.

9. External Links

Learnix may contain links to external websites, services or educational resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Users should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

10. Your Choices

You can control or delete cookies through the settings of your web browser. You may also clear locally stored website data from your browser settings.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you may stop using the Learnix website.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Learnix, technology, legal requirements or third-party services.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Learnix handles information, please contact us through the Contact page.

Visit the Learnix Contact Page

13. Consent

By using the Learnix website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
